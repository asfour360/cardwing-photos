# StackJet lounge photos

Resized copies (JPEG, longest side 1200 px) of the official lounge photos the StackJet app shows,
served with GitHub Pages so links don't break when an operator changes its website.

- `<network>/<airport>/<lounge>-<n>.jpg`
- `manifests/<network>.json` maps each lounge to its photos and their original official source URLs.

Only official photos from the lounge operators and airlines. Priority Pass lounges are not hosted here.

## Shared images (not official)

- `aircanada/shared/air-canada-maple-leaf-lounge-entrance-1.jpg`: an Air Canada Maple Leaf Lounge entrance (sign and maple-leaf roundel). Source: found on the web and supplied by the StackJet owner, who accepted the rights risk; not an official Air Canada photo and not taken at any one listed lounge. Used as a generic Air Canada lounge image for Air Canada lounges that have no photo of their own.
- `amex/yyc/aspire-amex-lounge-yyc-domestic-1.jpg` and `amex/yul/aspire-amex-lounge-yul-transborder-coming-soon-1.jpg`: the same official American Express photo of the Calgary (YYC) Aspire Amex Lounge, from American Express's September 16, 2026 newsroom release. The Montréal transborder lounge has not opened yet and has no photo of its own, so it shows the Calgary photo at the owner's request.
- `amex/bos/the-centurion-lounge-bos-coming-soon-1.jpg`, `amex/clt/sidecar-by-the-centurion-lounge-clt-coming-soon-5.jpg` and `amex/dfw/the-centurion-lounge-dfw-5.jpg`: American Express's own architectural renderings (not photos of finished rooms) of the planned Boston Logan Centurion Lounge entrance, the planned Sidecar by The Centurion Lounge entrance at Charlotte and the planned second bar at the expanded Dallas Fort Worth Centurion Lounge, from the American Express newsroom article "With 32 locations and growing, American Express expands its industry-leading lounge network".
- `aspire/ont/aspire-executive-lounge-terminal-2-ont-1.jpg` to `-3.jpg` (seating area, bar, reception): photos of the Aspire Executive Lounge in Terminal 2 at Ontario (ONT), supplied by the StackJet owner, not official Aspire photos. Metadata stripped.
- `amex/ams/the-centurion-lounge-ams-2.jpg` to `-5.jpg`: four photos of The Centurion Lounge at Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS) from American Express's own LoungeBuddy image host, copied here instead of hotlinked. Resized to 1200 px wide, metadata stripped.
